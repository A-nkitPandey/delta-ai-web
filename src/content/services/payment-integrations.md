A payment flow is only as good as its worst edge case. We verify every callback signature on the server, make each order idempotent so a double click never charges twice, and keep an audit trail you can hand to your accountant.

Need traffic split across gateways? We've built routing that alternates or balances gateways safely, decided inside the database so concurrent customers never collide.
