## The problem

Weekends meant a packed waiting area, customers leaving before their turn, and staff shouting names over the noise.

## What we built

A same-day token system that runs in the phone's browser — no app to install. Customers take a token, see who is being served now, get an approximate wait, and come back near their turn. Staff move the queue forward from a simple counter screen.

## How it's built

- Mobile-first web app, tuned for low-end phones and patchy networks
- Live queue updates without page refreshes
- Locked-down by default: strict referrer policy, security headers, no third-party trackers
