## The problem

Hiring ran on spreadsheets and email threads across several group companies. Managers couldn't see where a position stood, and HR spent hours chasing approvals.

## What we built

- **Hiring pipeline** — job requests, approvals, candidates, interviews and offers in one flow, with deadline-health tracking for every opening.
- **CV parsing and bulk import** — upload CVs and candidates are created automatically.
- **Everyday HR** — leave, tasks, company assets, helpdesk tickets and org charts.
- **Fine-grained permissions** — roles plus per-person exceptions where needed.
- **Live notifications** and sign-in with the group's existing Microsoft accounts.

## How it's built

- Supports several companies in the group on one system
- About 280 features and actions, covered by 108 automated tests
- Separate test and live environments with scripted, repeatable deployments
