## The problem

At busy hours, diners wait to get a menu, wait to order and wait again for the bill. Orders taken by hand get missed, and tourists or out-of-state guests struggle with the menu.

## What we built

- **Scan, order, pay** — each table has its own QR code. Diners browse the menu, order and pay by UPI, cards or wallets through Razorpay.
- **Live order tracking** — diners see when their food is being prepared and when it's on the way.
- **Kitchen & staff screen** — every order appears instantly, with kitchen-printer and WhatsApp alerts.
- **AI menu translation** — the menu can be shown in the guest's language automatically.
- **Owner tools** — menu, tables and staff management, plus a self-signup page for new restaurants.

## How it's built

- Fast web app that runs in any phone browser — nothing to download
- Each restaurant's data is kept separate and protected at the database level
- 282 automated tests, load-tested, with backups and a launch checklist
