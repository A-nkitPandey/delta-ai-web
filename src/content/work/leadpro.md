## The problem

Leads arrive from ads, calls, walk-ins and WhatsApp. Without one system, some are never called back — and nobody notices until the admission season is over.

## What we built

- **Ownership and next steps** — every lead has one owner, a next action and a due time.
- **Overdue escalation** — managers are alerted when a lead waits too long.
- **Automatic lead capture** — leads from ad platforms flow in without copy-pasting.
- **Response-time reports** — see how fast each team actually follows up.
- **Full audit trail** — who changed what, and when.

## How it's built

- Built for several companies on one system, with each company's data kept separate at two levels
- Two-factor sign-in required for owners and admins
- Modern, well-supported technology chosen for long-term maintenance
