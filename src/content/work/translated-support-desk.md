## The problem

Indian businesses serve customers in many languages, but support teams usually work in one or two. Customers either struggle in a language they're not comfortable with, or the business has to hire for every language.

## What we built

- **Translated chat** — each side types in their own language and reads the other's message translated.
- **Translated voice calls with subtitles** — speak naturally; the other person hears and reads it in their language.
- **Agent console** — incoming chats and calls go to the next free agent, with transfers between agents.
- **Admin portal** — manage teams, languages and how long recordings are kept.
- **One system, many businesses** — each company's conversations are kept separate.

## How it's built

- Real-time chat and calls in the browser — no app for the customer to install
- AI speech and translation services for Indian languages and English
- Recording-retention rules so data isn't kept longer than needed
