This policy explains what personal data Delta AI Innovation ("we") collects through this website and how we handle it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## What we collect

- **Contact form:** your name, email, company (optional), the service you're interested in, your budget range and your message.
- **Website chat:** the questions you type are sent to our AI assistant to answer them. We don't store them unless you choose to share them with our team.
- **WhatsApp:** if you message our WhatsApp number, our AI assistant reads your messages to reply, and we keep your number, name and the chat so our team can follow up.
- **Security data:** a one-way, salted hash of your IP address and your country, used only to block spam and abuse. We do not store your raw IP address.
- **No tracking cookies:** this website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

## Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the work you asked for. We never sell your data.

## Who processes it

- **Cloudflare** hosts this website, stores form submissions and WhatsApp chats, runs our AI assistant (Workers AI) and the Turnstile spam check.
- **Meta (WhatsApp)** delivers WhatsApp messages between you and us.
- **Resend** delivers the notification email that tells our team about your enquiry.

## How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't become projects are deleted within 12 months. WhatsApp chats stored by our assistant are deleted after 30 days.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time by emailing the address in the footer. We'll respond within 30 days.
